About Us

Founding
Genius Studio.

Genius Studio was founded in 2022 by Mohammed Hassan with a clear mission: to deliver exceptional solutions across the fields of technology, graphic design, motion graphics, original software development, and social media marketing.

Built on a foundation of creativity, innovation, and technical expertise, Genius Studio is committed to helping individuals, startups, and businesses elevate their digital presence. Whether it's developing cutting-edge software, designing compelling visuals, or crafting strategic marketing content, our goal is to turn ideas into impactful realities.

At Genius Studio, we don't just offer services — we build lasting partnerships rooted in quality, trust, and results.

With a multidisciplinary skill set, I serve as the Studio Manager, Lead Developer, Sales Assistant, as well as a Graphic and Motion Graphic Designer.

At Genius Studio, I combine my passion for technology and design to deliver innovative solutions and compelling visual content. Whether it's developing software, crafting brand identities, or producing dynamic motion graphics, I bring a hands-on, detail-oriented approach to every project.

Driven by creativity and a commitment to excellence, I strive to help clients bring their ideas to life through technology and design.
